What's new
----------
- visible model version number in the website header
- clean bathymetry background image instead of cluttered map tiles
- 5 m, 10 m, 15 m and 20 m depth contours shown on the background
- faint background percentage arrows across the bay
- checkbox options to show or hide RTYC buoy arrows and background arrows
- G and R buoy directions manually corrected to align with the established north-shore contour flow

Model basis
-----------
100% velocity = the deep-water flow outside the Berry Head–Hope's Nose line.
The site scales the relative percentages using the tide-range lookup from OpenWaters.

Not for navigation.
